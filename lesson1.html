Medical Terminology Basics

Medical terminology is the language healthcare professionals use to describe the human body, conditions, procedures, and treatments.

Word Parts

Most medical terms are made of three main parts:

  • Prefix: Beginning of a word that gives extra meaning
  • Root: The main meaning of the word
  • Suffix: Ending that describes a condition or procedure

Examples

Cardiology:

  • Cardio = heart
  • -logy = study of

Therefore: Cardiology = the study of the heart
